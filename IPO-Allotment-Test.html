IPO Allotment Success Calculator

Enter your PAN, pick how many random applicants compete with you, and see your allotment chance. Your data in. Your results out. Nothing stored.

Setup

Type any value you like, for example 10, 10000, 70L, 1.5cr or 7,50,000.

Each applicant is assumed to apply for one lot, and every valid application has an equal chance in the lottery. Other applicants are randomly generated unique PAN-style IDs. This is a practice tool, not an official allotment.

Result

Set your PAN and the number of applicants, then press Simulate.
